Privacy Policy

MyHermit · Last updated 4 October 2026

Short version: the developer of MyHermit collects nothing. The app has no accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs.

What the app talks to

MyHermit connects only to the Hermes Agent server whose address you enter. That server is yours; the developer does not operate it and cannot see its data. Your chats, cron jobs, credentials and settings travel between your device and your server and are stored on your server under your control.

Sign-in

Your dashboard password is sent only to your server, exchanged for a sign-in token, and never stored on the device. The token is kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only and is not synced.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the app sends its Apple push token to your server, which uses Apple Push Notification service to deliver alerts to you. The token is not sent to the developer.

Credentials you hand over

Values you enter in the Credentials tab are sent directly to your server and written where your agent asked for them. They are not kept by the app.

Demo mode

“Try the Demo” uses built-in sample data and does not connect to any Hermes server.

Children

MyHermit is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
