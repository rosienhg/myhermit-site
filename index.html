MyHermit

A simple iPhone and iPad app for the AI agent you run on your own server.

  • Chat with your agent, follow its tool calls live, and answer its questions.
  • Manage cron jobs, hand over credentials, and browse skills and plugins.
  • One-time purchase. No ads, no subscriptions, no analytics, no tracking.

Getting started

MyHermit is a client for a self-hosted AI agent server that you run yourself. Sign in with your agent's dashboard account (for example a http://….local address on your home network). The first time, the app offers to install and configure its companion plugin on your server. After that, restart the dashboard and you're done.

No server yet? Tap Try the Demo on the sign-in screen to look around with sample data.

Common questions

Sign-in says the dashboard has no password sign-in. Turn on password sign-in for the dashboard first; the app links to the instructions.

I can't connect from outside my home. MyHermit talks to your server directly. Plain http:// works only on your local network; from elsewhere, use an https:// address (for example through a VPN or a reverse proxy).

Notifications don't arrive. Check Settings → Notifications in the app and make sure the plugin is up to date under Settings → Server Setup.
